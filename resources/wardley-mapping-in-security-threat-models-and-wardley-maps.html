Wardley mapping in security — Threat Models and Wardley Maps

What it is

The single most credentialed gathering on Wardley mapping in security — Schoenfield, Tarandach and Cruz are established threat-modelling authorities, not mapping enthusiasts. The session explores "the use of Wardley Maps in Security, and how Wardley Maps could be the missing piece of the puzzle when doing Threat Models." The framing matters: the claim is not that maps replace threat models, but that threat modelling lacks a way to reason about where the landscape is going, and mapping supplies it. It is a working session rather than a lecture, so the recording is a genuine practitioner argument rather than a polished talk.

Limitations

From 2022. The recording link is stated but not verified — confirm before publishing. Working-session format means it is discursive and less immediately actionable than van Stein's written article. Licence unknown.
