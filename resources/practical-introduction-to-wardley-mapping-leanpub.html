Practical Introduction to Wardley Mapping (Leanpub)

No screenshot. Either this resource is not in the 30-target capture set, or the capture returned an interstitial rather than the page — which is recorded as a failure rather than published as a screenshot. What was and was not captured.

What it is

A 154-page step-by-step guide to creating and using Wardley maps, aimed at executives and managers. Covers user needs and customer journeys, map construction and component placement, strategic analysis and market evolution, and works a HomeChoice vs. Netflix case study. Includes workshop exercises. $14.99 suggested, free with Leanpub membership, in PDF and EPUB.

Limitations

Frozen in 2019 — predates the full doctrine grid, OnlineWardleyMaps' modern DSL, Mermaid support, and everything in the current tooling landscape. Not openly licensed, so nothing can be mirrored. Note the name collision: this is not Ben Mosior's material, despite "practical introduction" being a phrase people associate with Learn Wardley Mapping.
