Wardley Maps — the book (Medium serialisation)

Primary source Live book

#1 of the ten to read first. The primary source, and the chapter that establishes why before how. The CC BY-SA 4.0 licence on it is what makes the whole ecosystem exist.

What it is

The canonical text of the technique, serialised as 19 Medium chapters under the "Wardley Maps" publication. It moves from a narrative of strategic failure ("On Being Lost") through the construction of a map, to doctrine, climatic patterns, gameplay, scenario planning and organisational structure. It is deliberately autobiographical — Wardley describes his own failures as CEO of Fotango, the origin of the technique in 2005, and its refinement at Canonical. It is not a textbook; it is a long argument with worked examples, and it is the source every other resource in this list derives from. The full chapter list, verified: 1. Limitations

Long (572 pages in the compiled edition), digressive, and structurally uneven. Medium's reading experience is poor for a 19-part work; chapter 9's URL slug ("anticipation") doesn't match its title, which trips up automated indexers. It stops mid-argument — Wardley has said it is unfinished. No index, no glossary, and the diagrams are inline images with no alt text.
