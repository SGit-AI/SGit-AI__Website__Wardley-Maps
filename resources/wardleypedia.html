What it is

"The free strategy encyclopedia that anyone can edit" — an attempt at a canonical reference wiki for Wardley mapping terminology, patterns and tools. Its Climatic patterns page is one of the few dedicated, structured treatments of the climatic pattern list outside the book itself, and its tool pages (Atlas2 among them) preserve history that has otherwise vanished.

Limitations

Fourteen articles. It has been overtaken by learnwardleymapping.com's glossary and reference sections, which are better written and better maintained. No visible editorial activity, unstated licence, and a MediaWiki URL scheme (/mediawiki/index.php/...) that is fragile.
