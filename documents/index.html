The documents

The research this site was built from, published in full. Ten documents, 17,321 words, plus a 73-row source manifest. The raw markdown in /briefs/ is the source of truth — the reader pages render those same files rather than copying them, so the two cannot drift.

Publishing the brief alongside the site is not generosity, it is the same discipline as shipping a map's source next to its render and naming the artefact behind every doctrine rating. It also lets you catch us: in two places the research disagrees with the artefacts it describes, this site publishes the artefacts' numbers, and you can now check which is right — N1 and N2.

And the data

Three of these documents ship structured data alongside the prose. It is served as-is:

  • /data/industry-resources.json — 34 resources and 11 dead entries, each with licence and a pre-written attribution string. The pages built from it →
  • /data/dinis-published.json — 26 published items with date-precision and verification flags. The chronology →
  • /agents/concepts.json — 35 concept records, parsed from the concepts index rather than retyped. The glossary →
  • /doctrine/doctrine.json — 40 doctrine records with evidence links. The assessment →
  • /screenshots/targets.json and captured.json — the 30 capture targets with their licence strings, and what actually happened to each.

All of it CC BY 4.0. Every markdown document carries the stamp, and the release gate fails the build if one does not.
